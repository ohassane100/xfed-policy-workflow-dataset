# XFed standards alignment

This package keeps the existing XFed pipeline unchanged.

## ODRL
Used for the machine-readable policy rules in `05_policy_schema.json`: permissions, prohibitions, duties/obligations, parties, actions, targets, and constraints.

Reference: https://www.w3.org/TR/odrl-model/

## OSDU
Used for energy-domain legal/compliance metadata in the optional `osdu` object of `05_policy_schema.json`.

- `osdu.legalTag` follows OSDU Legal Service LegalTag field names: `countryOfOrigin`, `contractId`, `expirationDate`, `dataType`, `originator`, `securityClassification`, `exportClassification`, `personalData`, and `extensionProperties`.
- `osdu.legal` follows OSDU record-level legal metadata: `legaltags` and `otherRelevantDataCountries`.
- OSDU's system-generated legal/compliance `status` is intentionally not generated by XFed.
- Allowed LegalTag values can be data-partition specific, so deployment-time validation against the OSDU Legal Service remains necessary.

Reference: https://osdu.pages.opengroup.org/platform/security-and-compliance/legal/api/

## Resulting XFed flow

`contract -> contract_chunks.json -> policy.json (ODRL + compact OSDU metadata) -> policy review -> workflow.yaml`
