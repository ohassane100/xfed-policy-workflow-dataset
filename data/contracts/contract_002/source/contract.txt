DATA COLLABORATION AGREEMENT
Offshore Equipment Monitoring Demonstration

Parties:

Company A:
NorthSea Operations AS
Role: Data Owner and Host Company

Company B:
Fjord Analytics AS
Role: Analysis Partner

1. Purpose

Company B may analyse Company A's offshore compressor telemetry solely for predictive-maintenance and anomaly-detection purposes.

Company B may not use the data for unrelated commercial purposes, general-purpose model training, or profiling of Company A's employees.

2. Data Location

All raw compressor telemetry, equipment identifiers, exact timestamps, maintenance records, and raw sensor rows shall remain inside Company A's computing environment.

Company B shall not download, copy, export, or otherwise transfer raw data outside Company A's environment.

3. Computation

Company B may submit approved analysis code for execution inside Company A's controlled computing environment.

Company A must provide a controlled execution environment in which the approved analysis can run against the relevant dataset.

Company B shall not execute unapproved software against Company A's data.

4. Permitted Outputs

Company B may receive:
- aggregated anomaly counts;
- feature statistics;
- sanitized event patterns;
- threshold summaries.

Company B may not receive:
- raw sensor rows;
- exact equipment identifiers;
- exact event timestamps;
- maintenance records containing confidential operational details.

5. Output Approval

Company A must review and approve outputs before they are released from Company A's environment to Company B.

Company B shall not bypass the output approval process.

6. Confidentiality

Both companies must protect confidential information obtained through the collaboration.

Company B shall not disclose Company A's data or confidential outputs to third parties without Company A's written approval.

7. Logging

Company A must maintain a record of analyses executed against the dataset and outputs released to Company B.

Company B must identify the purpose of each submitted analysis.

8. Termination

When the collaboration ends, Company B must delete any temporary permitted outputs that it is no longer authorised to retain.

The obligation to protect confidential information continues after termination.
