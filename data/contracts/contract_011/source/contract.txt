                            Completing the Data Access Agreement
                            This data access agreement form is to be completed by the Principal Researcher
                            requesting access to the Growing Up in New Zealand External Working Data Set.
                            Where co -researchers are work ing on the same piece of research, these individuals must
                            countersign the form.
                            All researchers must read the document titled Growing Up in New ZealandData Access
                            Protocol and under take the necessar y preparation and checks before submitting the
                            data access agreement.
                            A scanned copy of the completed form needs to be sent to dataaccess@growingup.
