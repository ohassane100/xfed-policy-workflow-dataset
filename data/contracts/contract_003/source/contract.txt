DATA COLLABORATION AGREEMENT
 Small Monitoring Demo
Parties
 Company A: NorthSea Operations AS (data owner and host)
Company B: Fjord Analytics AS (analysis partner)
1. Purpose
Company B may analyze Company A's compressor telemetry only to detect anomalies.
2. Data Location
Company A must keep raw compressor telemetry inside its computing environment.
Company B may not export raw compressor telemetry outside that environment.
3. Outputs
Company B may receive only aggregated anomaly counts that Company A has approved.
4. Confidentiality
Both parties must protect confidential information from this collaboration.
